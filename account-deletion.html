Account Deletion

Effective Date: [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

You can delete your Gustus account at any time. This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data when you do.

How to Delete Your Account

Method 1: In the App (Recommended)

  1. Open the Gustus app
  2. Navigate to Settings → Account → Delete account
  3. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm deletion
  4. Type "DELETE" when prompted to confirm

Method 2: By Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by email:

  1. Send an email to [SUPPORT_EMAIL]
  2. Use the email address associated with your Gustus account
  3. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line
  4. Specify that you want to permanently delete your account

We will process your request within a reasonable timeframe (typically within 30 days) and confirm deletion once completed.

What Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Account information:
    • Email address
    • Display name
    • Avatar URL
    • User ID and authentication identifiers
  • All user-generated content:
    • All coffee brew logs and recipes
    • All ratings, notes, and sensory evaluations
    • All taste tags and feeling tags
    • All brew method details and recipe parameters
    • All visibility settings

Warning: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Once your account is deleted, all your data will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered. Please ensure you have exported any data you wish to keep before proceeding.

What May Remain Temporarily

After account deletion, the following may remain for a limited period due to technical requirements:

  • Backups: Database backups may contain your data until backup retention expires. We do not maintain backups of deleted data long-term.
  • Security logs: Operational logs that reference your account ID (but not personal content like notes or ratings) may be retained according to standard log retention policies for security and debugging purposes.
  • Supabase Auth records: In rare cases where Supabase Auth deletion fails, your authentication record may remain in Supabase's systems, but you will not be able to access the app as your application data will be deleted.

Deletion Process

Account deletion is performed immediately upon confirmation. The deletion process:

  • Removes your user profile from the database
  • Cascades to delete all associated coffee logs and related data
  • Attempts to delete your authentication record from Supabase
  • Occurs within a single database transaction to ensure data consistency

You will be signed out of the app immediately after deletion. If you try to sign in again with the same Google account, you will need to create a new account.
