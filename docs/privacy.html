1. Who We Are

Gustus is a social coffee journal mobile application operated by Eleftherios Delimitrou. For questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at eldelielev@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following types of data when you use Gustus:

Account Data

  • Email address: Collected when you sign in with Google via Supabase
  • Display name: Your name from your Google profile
  • Avatar URL: Profile picture URL from your Google profile
  • Authentication identifiers: User ID and provider information managed by Supabase Auth

User-Generated Content

When you use Gustus to log your coffee experiences, we store:

  • Coffee brew logs including recipe details (dose, yield, temperature, time, grind settings, brew method, device)
  • Ratings and sensory evaluations (body, sweetness, bitterness, acidity, clarity, aftertaste)
  • Taste tags and feeling tags
  • Free-text notes including aroma notes, taste notes, and cooling notes
  • Visibility settings for your brews (private or public)

Technical and Log Data

We collect limited operational logs necessary for the app to function, including authentication events and API requests. These logs do not contain personal content such as your notes or ratings.

3. How We Use Data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Authenticate your account and provide secure access to the app
  • Store and display your coffee logs, ratings, and notes
  • Enable you to view and manage your brewing history
  • Allow you to share your brews publicly (if you choose to make them visible)
  • Maintain app security and prevent unauthorized access
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries

4. Sharing and Third-Party Processing

We share your data with the following third-party service providers:

Supabase

  • Purpose: Authentication and database hosting
  • Data processed: All account data, authentication credentials, and user-generated content
  • Location: Supabase cloud infrastructure
  • For more information, see Supabase's Privacy Policy

Google

  • Purpose: OAuth authentication for sign-in
  • Data processed: Email address, display name, and avatar URL from your Google account
  • For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise monetize your personal data. We only share data as necessary to provide the service and as described in this policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data and user-generated content for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will permanently delete:

  • Your user profile (email, display name, avatar URL)
  • All your coffee brew logs and associated recipe details
  • All your ratings, notes, and tags

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Data cannot be recovered after deletion. Backups may contain deleted data for a short period until backup retention expires, but we do not maintain backups of deleted data long-term.

For instructions on how to delete your account, please see our Account Deletion Policy.

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Go to Settings → Account → Delete account
  • By email: Contact us at eldelielev@gmail.com from your account email address

For detailed instructions, please see our Account Deletion Policy.

7. Security

We implement security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption in transit using TLS/HTTPS for all data transmission
  • Access controls and authentication to ensure only authorized users can access their data
  • Database-level security including row-level security policies

While we take reasonable steps to secure your data, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use strong authentication and do not share your account credentials.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can view all your data within the Gustus app
  • Correction: You can update your profile information and edit your coffee logs at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete individual brews or your entire account at any time
  • Data Portability: Contact us at eldelielev@gmail.com to request a copy of your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at eldelielev@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Gustus is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us at eldelielev@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete that information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page and, where appropriate, through in-app notifications. Your continued use of Gustus after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at eldelielev@gmail.com.
